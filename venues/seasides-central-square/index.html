Saturday, Sep 26

  • Seaside Farmers Market
    Seaside · Sat, Sep 26 · 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM · Free

    Join us at the SEASIDE® Farmers Market, a beloved weekly tradition in Seaside, Florida, held in the Amphitheater from 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM. This outdoor market brings together local farmers, growers, and artisans, offering fresh produce, baked goods, and specialty foods. Browse the market, support local vendors, and enjoy live music in the heart of Seaside. This event is rain or shine, unless inclement weather creates unsafe conditions.
